MOONX Rug Pull
by GhostChain 14 March 2026 8 min read

The MOONX token launched on 9 March 2026 with aggressive influencer marketing across Twitter and Telegram. Within 72 hours it had accumulated $12M in TVL. By the morning of the 12th, the liquidity was gone.

Our chain sleuth GhostChain began tracking the deployer wallet 48 hours before the rug. The wallet had prior history with two other failed launches under different contract addresses — a pattern our database flagged immediately.

The Mechanism

  • Developer wallet was pre-loaded with 18% of supply via 7 proxy wallets
  • Liquidity pool was never locked — a one-line contract check reveals this
  • Exit funds routed through a Tornado Cash-adjacent mixer on Arbitrum
  • Influencers were paid $4,000–$12,000 per post — 3 have since deleted their accounts
  • Final destination wallet traced to a known OTC desk in Southeast Asia

This investigation has been submitted to relevant authorities. The full wallet trace is available in our public investigation archive.
